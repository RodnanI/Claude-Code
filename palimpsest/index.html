Palimpsest

an atlas of worlds that never were

Speak a word, and a world will be drawn from it: its coasts and rivers, the peoples who come to live there, their tongues and their letters, and a thousand years of what they did to one another, kept by the scribes who lived through it.

Nothing on this map is ever quite erased. Old borders stay faint under new ones, and a town keeps its old name, struck through, under the one its conquerors gave it.

The same word always gives the same world.
